# EcoTrace Sponsorship and Partnership Guide

Thank you for your interest in supporting **EcoTrace**, an open-source, lightweight carbon footprint and energy estimation framework designed for sustainable software development.

EcoTrace is built to bridge developer workflows with empirical carbon accounting. We welcome individual backers, academic research labs, and enterprise partners.

---

## Partnership Tracks

We categorize our backing into three primary tracks:

### 1. Academic and Research Partnerships
We collaborate with universities, research institutes, and independent labs working on Green Computing, High-Performance Computing (HPC), Green AI, and Distributed Systems.

* **What we offer:**
  * Access to the EcoTrace engine, VS Code instrumentation layer, and raw measurement logs.
  * Joint co-authorship on academic papers, conference publications, and technical whitepapers.
  * Prominent placement of your university or laboratory logo on our documentation and README as an Official Academic Partner.
* **What we seek:**
  * Empirical validation of our mathematical and power consumption models against laboratory equipment (RAPL, IPMI, physical wattmeters).
  * Joint grant applications (e.g., Horizon Europe, TUBITAK 1001/1505, national scientific research funds).

### 2. Infrastructure and Cloud Sponsors
EcoTrace runs continuous integration, cross-platform telemetry tests (Linux, macOS, Windows), and grid intensity database sync.

* **What we offer:**
  * "Infrastructure Powered by [Your Organization]" badge on our documentation and GitHub repository.
  * Direct case studies highlighting your cloud or datacenter efficiency.
* **What we seek:**
  * Cloud compute credits (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Hetzner, Equinix Metal).
  * Access to diverse CPU and GPU architectures (ARM64, Apple Silicon, NVIDIA/AMD GPUs) for hardware power profiling.

### 3. Enterprise and Institutional Backers
For companies committed to Scope 3 emissions reporting, ESG compliance, and sustainable engineering cultures.

* **What we offer:**
  * Top-tier logo display on the EcoTrace website and README.
  * Custom integration consulting and priority technical support.
  * Quarterly roadmap input and feature request prioritization.
* **What we seek:**
  * Monthly or annual financial grants to sustain full-time development, security audits, and maintenance.
